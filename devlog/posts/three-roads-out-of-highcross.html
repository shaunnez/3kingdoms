001 / 2026-09-30 / Game concept

Three roads out of Highcross.

A shared home connects Fantasy, Science and Chaos. What you discover in one realm can change how you understand another.

Approved concept baseline

The starting idea comes from 3 Kingdoms MUD: a familiar social home, wildly different realms, guilds that change how you play, and mysteries that reward attention. THREEFOLD translates that structure into an original, cinematic isometric browser RPG.

An abandoned bell remembers being a machine. A prediction terminal describes a death that has not happened. A carnival performer wears someone else's shadow. These connections are the reason to keep exploring, beyond the next level or item.

Two requirements are fixed: all twelve guilds belong in the game, and players can fight one another anywhere outside towns and designated safe zones. Exploration, preparation, retreat and returning home all have consequences.

What we can verify

The concept baseline records the full guild roster and the user's open-PvP rule.

Still ahead

This is a design milestone. A playable world and production assets have not been built.
